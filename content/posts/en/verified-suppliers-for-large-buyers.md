A supplier once showed up with 10 tons of rice. No documentation, no prior validation. Just a truck and a phone number.

The buyer had a purchase order, a deadline and a lot of pressure from above. They had to make a call: trust the supplier or walk away.

## A decision made blind

In that moment there was no system to verify who the supplier was. There was no transaction history to check, and no way to know whether the rice would meet spec, whether the weight was accurate, or whether the money would be safe.

So the buyer took a gamble. Most buyers in Indonesia's agri supply chain do, every day.

The supplier turned out to be legitimate. He was hard-working and had been farming for 20 years. He simply had no way to prove it, and no system that gave him the chance to.

## Where the risk lives

Indonesia's agricultural supply chain is one of the largest in Southeast Asia, and one of the hardest for large buyers to navigate. Most of the risk sits in the informal first mile: unverified suppliers, undocumented transactions and no recourse when things go wrong.

That moment, a buyer with a deadline facing a supplier with no records, is the reason we built PasarMIKRO's Trade & Trade Finance services.
