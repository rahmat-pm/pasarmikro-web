We used to hear one line a lot: smallholder farmers are too risky to finance. Honestly, we understood why people said it.

## Why the belief exists

There is no credit history, no formal records and no paper trail to follow when things go wrong. From the outside, lending into Indonesia's agri sector can feel like stepping into the unknown.

Years on the ground taught us something different. The risk was never really about the farmers. It was about the lack of tools to actually know them.

So PasarMIKRO built those tools. Responsible financing in agriculture needs more than capital. It needs strong risk and credit foundations: visibility, discipline and trust.

## What our Credit & Risk Services cover

Our [Credit & Risk Services](/solutions/risk-credit/) help financial institutions, development organizations, corporate partners and investors see what was previously invisible across agricultural and fishery value chains. They cover the full risk lifecycle:

- **Due diligence and onboarding.** KYC, CLIK and adverse media checks on every business in our network, plus UBO and credit bureau checks and document verification.
- **Financial and operational due diligence.** A genuine assessment, not box-ticking.
- **Credit risk assessments.** Grounded in business models, trade flows and repayment capacity.
- **Onsite verification and inspections.** Our [field team](/solutions/field-verification/) shows up in person across key production regions in Java, Sumatra, Bali and beyond.
- **Transaction-based monitoring.** Portfolio and transactional risk monitoring, in near real time, for partners transacting on the PasarMIKRO platform, so you are never flying blind.
- **Structured reporting and oversight.** For funders and development institutions.

## Who does the work

The services are delivered by an experienced team of former bankers, auditors, risk professionals, data analysts and field officers, with field officers embedded across key production regions.

What makes the work hold up is not the framework alone. It is the combination of:

- verified data, including credit bureau data
- structured processes and disciplined controls
- strong governance standards
- real transactions linked to real commodities

## Standalone or with the platform

You can use these services on their own, or integrated with the PasarMIKRO platform. When partners transact on the platform, the services go further: ongoing monitoring, early warning signals and transparent reporting.

Either way, the aim is better visibility, earlier risk signals and more confidence in financing and trade decisions.

For us, risk management is not about saying no. It is about enabling responsible growth, protecting portfolios, and helping agri and fishery micro-entrepreneurs, our #MikroHeroes, access financing that is sustainable for everyone involved.
