Eid al-Adha is a moment of sacrifice, gratitude and shared blessings. Those values also sit at the heart of what we do at PasarMIKRO.

## The breeders behind Qurban season

Every year, millions of livestock change hands across Indonesia in the lead-up to Qurban season. Behind every transaction is a smallholder breeder who has spent months, sometimes years, raising cattle, goats and sheep, often without access to fair pricing or formal financing.

This is the gap PasarMIKRO was built to close.

## What we offer

- Transparent, fair pricing.
- Access to working capital, so smallholders can operate and grow with dignity.
- Transactions that are traceable, secure and designed to put more value back into the hands of producers.

## Shared prosperity

As the spirit of Eid al-Adha reminds us, true prosperity is shared. A supply chain that works for everyone, from the breeder to the buyer, is how that spirit becomes something real and lasting.
