Selamat Hari Raya Idul Fitri 1447 H. On behalf of the whole PasarMIKRO family, we send our warmest wishes to everyone celebrating. May this Eid bring joy, peace and prosperity to you and your loved ones.

## Looking back on the year

As Ramadan ends, we take a moment to reflect on the past year. We have grown in scale, in impact and in our commitment to micro and small businesses across Indonesia.

None of that would have been possible without the trust and collaboration of our shareholders, investors and partners. To everyone who has placed their confidence in PasarMIKRO: thank you. We do not take your partnership lightly.

## Building what comes next, together

Eid is a time of renewal, and we enter the next chapter with a clear sense of purpose.
