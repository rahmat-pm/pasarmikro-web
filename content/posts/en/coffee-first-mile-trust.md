The coffee in your cup has probably passed through 5 middlemen before it reached you, and the farmer who grew it may have seen less than 3% of what you paid.

## A trust problem, not a supply problem

This is not really a supply chain problem. It is a trust problem.

There is no visibility, no verification and no recourse. There are only layers of informal transactions that wear away value at every step of the way.

The global agri industry keeps operating as if this were inevitable. It is not.

## The supply and the demand are already there

Indonesia alone has millions of smallholder farmers producing some of the world's most sought-after commodities: rice, coffee, corn, tuna and natural fibre.

The supply is there. So is the demand. What has been missing is the infrastructure to connect them fairly and transparently.
