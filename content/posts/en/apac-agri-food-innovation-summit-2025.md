Hugo Verwayen, Co-Founder and CEO of PasarMIKRO, will speak on the Main Stage at the Asia-Pacific Agri-Food Innovation Summit in Singapore on 6 November 2025 at 11:40 (SGT). The summit runs from 4 to 6 November 2025.

## The panel

Hugo joins the panel "Innovative Agri-Fintech: How is Digital Finance Improving Rural Livelihoods in Asia-Pacific?" alongside:

- Prasun Kumar Das, Secretary General, Asia-Pacific Rural and Agricultural Credit Association (APRACA)
- Hilman Simanjuntak, President Director, PT Zurich General Takaful Indonesia (Zurich Insurance)
- Benedikt Signer, Executive Director, Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Insurance Facility (SEADRIF)
- Michael Walz, Vice President, Technical Sales Lead Public Sector Solutions, Swiss Re

## Why we are taking part

We are glad to represent Indonesia's growing agri-fintech ecosystem, and to show how digital finance, payment certainty, and embedded trade finance and insurance are improving rural livelihoods while making supply chains traceable and sustainable.

If you are attending the summit in Singapore, join us at the session.
