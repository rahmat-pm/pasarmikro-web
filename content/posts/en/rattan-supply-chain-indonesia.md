Indonesia is home to the world's largest rattan industry and supplies approximately 80% to 85% of global raw material demand. Yet most of its collectors still earn below market value, and many crafters still struggle with traceability, efficiency and certification readiness.

## Why rattan matters

Rattan is sustainable by nature: it grows fast, it can be harvested without clearing forest, and it stores carbon. It is one of the few commodities where environmental responsibility and commercial opportunity really line up.

## What holds it back

Without a reliable supply chain, that opportunity stays out of reach. Producers earn less than they could, and buyers cannot count on consistent supply.

Many producers still work with manual, fragmented processes, while buyer and audit requirements keep growing.
