Our view: by 2027, agri exporters without verified traceability will not only face compliance risk. They risk being priced out of premium markets.

## The data gap

There are no official figures on EUDR readiness among Indonesian farmers. By one estimate, as of mid-2024, less than 5% of Indonesian smallholder farmers had geo-referenced polygon data uploaded.

That matters because geolocation data for every plot is a basic EUDR requirement. Our [EUDR page](/eudr/) explains what is needed for each plot.

## EUDR is close, and buyers are already acting

The EUDR applies from 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators and traders, and from 30 June 2027 for micro and small operators.

Buyers across Europe are not waiting for those dates. They are quietly building supplier shortlists around one question: can you prove it?

## Why most supply chains cannot answer yet

Most agri supply chains in Southeast Asia still cannot answer that question. Not because the data does not exist, but because it was never captured, structured or made auditable.

## ESG needs field-level data

ESG reporting without field-level data is just storytelling. Dashboards that do not connect to real transactions are theater. Carbon numbers without source verification are noise.
