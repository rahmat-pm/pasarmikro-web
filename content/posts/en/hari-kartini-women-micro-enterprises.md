We mark Hari Kartini not only as a symbol of women's emancipation, but as a call to widen fair access and opportunity.

## The women at the foundation of the economy

Millions of women-led micro-enterprises sit at the foundation of Indonesia's economy. They play two roles at once: they drive household income, and they strengthen the resilience of their communities.

Yet many of them still have limited access to:

- financing,
- markets,
- business support.

## What PasarMIKRO is building

PasarMIKRO is committed to closing that gap. We are building an ecosystem that connects micro-entrepreneurs, women in particular, to wider market access, digital tools and opportunities for sustainable growth.

For us, Kartini's spirit today is about scaling with impact: making sure business growth goes hand in hand with measurable social outcomes.

## Why we focus on women in the micro sector

We believe that supporting women in the micro sector has far-reaching effects. It raises income, and we believe it also improves children's education, family health and the resilience of local economies.
