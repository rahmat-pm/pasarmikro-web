"The process is fast and simple, and the quick disbursement helps maintain healthy cash flow so I can focus on growing my business."

That is Bella Fajar, a coffee trader in Medan, describing what changed after no longer having to wait weeks to get paid.

## What the 2025 figures show

Bella Fajar's trades are among the more than 25,000 digital trades now completed on PasarMIKRO. Our 2025 Impact Report shows what they add up to:

- USD 83M+ in transactions facilitated
- 7,800+ registered users across 100+ districts
- 62% of our main platform users are women

## Where PasarMIKRO fits

We are not the lender, the insurer or the commodity trader. We are the digital infrastructure that connects Indonesia's farmers, fisherfolk and traders to finance, trusted markets and traceable supply chains.
