The global rattan furniture market is worth billions, yet the women who weave it work almost entirely off the grid. For buyers and brands committed to responsible sourcing, that is more than a social issue. It is a traceability gap.

## Who does the work

Homeweavers in Indonesia's rattan hubs work as subcontracted labor. They have:

- no formal employment,
- no transaction records,
- no access to financing.

## What buyers are now asked to show

International markets increasingly expect supply chain transparency down to the production unit. ESG compliance, due diligence regulations and impact reporting frameworks all point the same way: you need to know who made the product, and under what conditions.

When the people who make it leave no records, there is nothing to trace.

## Closing the gap at the weaver level

PasarMIKRO works on this at ground level. Our platform digitizes the subcontractor and weaver layer of the supply chain:
