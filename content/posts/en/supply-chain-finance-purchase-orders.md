Across emerging markets, SMEs face a frustrating paradox. They have confirmed purchase orders. They have committed buyers. The demand is there.

Yet production stalls. Not because they lack market access, but because they lack liquidity.

## Why traditional financing falls short

Traditional financing tools do not mirror conditions on the ground. They demand fixed assets and rigid collateral.

When an SME secures a large purchase order, traditional lenders rarely accept that contract as security to cover raw materials and labor upfront. The cash does not arrive until after delivery. That structural mismatch holds back growth.

## Financing built on real trade

This is where supply chain finance makes the difference. Instead of relying on physical collateral, we structure financing around verified transactions, purchase orders and buyer relationships. Capital moves in step with real economic activity.

In practice it works in four steps:

1. **Purchase order confirmed.** The buyer confirms demand, and the transaction is verified and digitized on the platform.
2. **Finance structured.** The financing is built around transaction data and buyer relationships, not fixed collateral.
3. **Capital deployed upfront.** The SME can pay for raw materials, labor and production without waiting for the buyer to settle.
4. **Credit history built.** Every recorded transaction adds to a data-backed track record.

## A credit history, not just a loan

The last step matters most. By digitizing and recording verified transactions, our system helps SMEs build a solid credit history.

We are not only providing temporary liquidity. We are building the track record SMEs need to become bankable in the long run.

## What changes for SMEs and buyers

- Less cash flow volatility
- More reliable fulfillment for buyers
- A clear, data-backed path to long-term bankability
- More resilient supply chains
