PasarMIKRO is a digital platform for trading agricultural commodities, built for smallholder farmers, traders and the businesses that buy from them in Indonesia.

We help #MikroHeroes run their business digitally: simpler transactions, faster payment, and a credit limit that can grow as you keep trading with us.
