---
question: "How do I join PasarMIKRO?"
order: 70
---

Register in the app, then verify your account. We verify every account so we know each user's identity and business profile before they trade.

**Register your account**

1. Download the [PasarMIKRO app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.altermyth.usahatelur).
2. Fill in your personal details and a few basic questions.
3. Enter the OTP code sent to your phone number.

**Verify your account**

1. Tap the "complete data" menu.
2. Fill in your bank account number and identity number.
3. Upload a photo of your ID.
