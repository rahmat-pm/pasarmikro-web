You get safer transactions, faster payment, a credit rating, a wide trading network and simple bookkeeping, all in one app.

- **Safer transactions.** Our field team surveys users before they trade, so you know who you are dealing with.
- **Direct payment.** Buyers get funding during the trading cycle, so sellers are paid directly. See [how Talangin works](/solutions/embedded-finance/).
- **Credit rating.** Every user of the Talangin facility gets a credit rating from the PasarMIKRO Risk team.
- **Wide network.** Buyers and sellers find the commodities they need from users across Indonesia.
- **Bookkeeping.** No more manual records: use Manage Stock, Financial Records and other features in the app.
